# Ninety Sense — channel style profile

## Audience and promise
A psychologically curious audience interested in overthinking, relationships, identity, loneliness, behavior, inner conflict, and uncomfortable emotional truths.

## Editorial identity
Dark, cinematic psychological storytelling. The mood should feel intentional and emotionally specific, not like generic AI motivation content.

## Visual language
- Build scenes around the actual emotional idea in the narration.
- Use dramatic but controlled lighting, meaningful environments, silhouettes, close details, reflections, empty spaces, shadows, and symbolic objects.
- Maintain character and environment continuity when a recurring character is used.
- Use visual metaphors to make internal states visible.
- Avoid random luxury imagery, generic sad-person montages, and repetitive dark-room shots.
- Character references supplied by the user should be respected for continuity.

## Typography and emphasis
- Minimal, forceful on-screen text.
- Use one strong hook and selective short emphasis phrases.
- Time animated text to the spoken phrase.
- Do not turn every sentence into a caption animation.
- Keep mobile readability and platform safe areas in mind.

## Sound
- Music should create tension, introspection, unease, or emotional release according to the story.
- Use silence, breathing room, subtle ambience, and selective sound effects.
- Avoid constant whooshes, impacts, and overdramatic effects.
- Voiceover remains the emotional and narrative center.

## Story rules
- Make the viewer recognize a specific feeling or behavior.
- Prefer concrete observations and contradictions over generic advice.
- Do not diagnose viewers or present speculation as psychological fact.
- Do not use fake quotations or attribute invented ideas to psychologists or books.
- End with a memorable realization, unresolved question, or emotional turn.

## Format adaptation
For vertical video, keep the focal subject and key text in the central mobile-safe area. For horizontal video, use environmental space and deliberate negative space without weakening the emotional focus.
