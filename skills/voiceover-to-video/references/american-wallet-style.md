# American Wallet — channel profile

## Audience and promise
US personal-finance audience, especially older adults and people approaching or living in retirement. Explain financial consequences clearly and respectfully.

## Visual identity
- Brand colors only: Deep Navy `#14263D` and Warm Ivory `#F5F1E8`.
- Cinematic financial documentary language: realistic environments, documents, objects, maps, timelines, and accurate explanatory graphics.
- Use charts only when real data is supplied and the chart clarifies the story.
- Preserve the supplied host reference's identity and recognizable appearance when provided. Do not substitute a generic younger presenter.
- Do not apply Ninety Sense's 3D cartoon / 2D face visual system.

## Story and text
Start with a real financial concern, question, or consequence. Explain what changes, who is affected, when it matters, and what the viewer should understand. Politics may be context or a hook when relevant, but finance remains the subject. Use large readable text and selective emphasis, especially for dollar amounts and dates.

## Sound
Use restrained documentary music and motivated environmental effects. Keep narration clear and dominant. Avoid sensational music that exaggerates the evidence.

## Integrity
Never invent financial statistics, current events, policy details, account balances, government forms, official seals, or financial advice. Do not add a subscribe prompt unless requested.
